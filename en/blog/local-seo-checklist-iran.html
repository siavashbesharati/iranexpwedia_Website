A local SEO checklist for Iranian businesses

If your customers are in your city or neighbourhood, local SEO is the fastest route to visibility. A checklist you can run this week.

For a clinic, a training centre, a real estate agency or any local service, competing on broad terms like “best clinic” is close to pointless. What actually produces calls is local intent: “skin clinic in Saadat Abad”, “laptop repair near me”. The good news is that competition on those searches is far lower.

First: make your basic details identical everywhere

Your name, address and phone number must be written exactly the same way on your site, on Maps, on social profiles and in directories. Small differences — “Unit 6” versus “3rd floor” — make it harder for a search engine to be sure it is the same business.

Second: one page per service, per area

A single “our services” page listing ten things ranks for none of them. If you have three core services across two areas, you need separate pages, each answering one specific need — and they should link to each other so the structure is legible.

Third: take Maps seriously

A large share of local calls comes from the map, not from the blue links. Complete the profile: real opening hours, the correct category, recent photos of the premises and replies to every review. An abandoned profile with wrong hours sends customers straight to a competitor.

Fourth: collect reviews systematically

Reviews affect both rankings and decisions, but waiting for them does not work. Build a simple path: after each successful job, send a short message with a direct link to the review page. With a CRM or unified inbox, this can be automated.

  • Send the request 24 hours after the service, not immediately.
  • Give a direct link; “search for us on Google” means nobody does it.
  • Answer negative reviews within 24 hours, without arguing.

Fifth: add structured data

LocalBusiness schema tells search engines what you are, where you are and when you are open. It is a one-time job that changes how your result is displayed. If you have an FAQ page, FAQ schema also increases the chance of answers being shown directly.

Sixth: speed and mobile

Local searches happen on phones, often on the move. A slow page costs you both ranking and the visitor. Keeping the page light matters more than any SEO trick.

How do you know it worked?

Record three numbers monthly: calls from Maps, messages from the site, and searches that include your brand name. Growth in the third means you are becoming known; growth in the first two means that recognition is turning into customers.
