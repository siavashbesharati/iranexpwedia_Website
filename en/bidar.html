The workflow

From message to lead in four steps

  1. A customer messages youFrom WhatsApp, Divar or Bale — it lands in the team’s shared inbox instantly.
  2. The first reply lands immediatelyThe assistant answers using your business knowledge, clarifies the need and keeps the lead warm.
  3. Handover at the right momentWhen it gets serious or sensitive, it is tagged “needs a human” and routed to the right operator.
  4. Lead, task and campaignThe number and history are stored, a follow-up task is created and the contact is ready for future campaigns.

Frequently asked questions

What exactly does Bidar do?

It unifies WhatsApp, Divar and Bale in one inbox, replies with AI assistance, turns each conversation into a tagged lead and adds tasks, campaigns and reporting.

How long does setup take?

Connecting channels usually happens in the same session. Training the assistant on your documents and pricing takes one or two working sessions depending on volume.

Where do the AI answers come from? Can it answer wrongly?

The assistant only uses the knowledge you give it: your service list, pricing and frequent questions. For sensitive topics, auto-reply is disabled and the chat is tagged “needs a human” for an operator.

Where is our customer data stored?

Data is kept in your own business workspace with per-operator access control. Exact retention terms are set out in the contract.

Is there support after launch?

Yes. Team training is part of onboarding, and afterwards you can take monthly support or run the system yourself.

Is it worth it for a two-person sales team?

If you get more than 30 messages a day, yes — unanswered messages are the biggest hidden cost for small teams. Below that volume we usually suggest starting with another service.
