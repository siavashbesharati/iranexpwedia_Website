We started with delivery work for Iranian businesses: websites, SEO, content and campaigns. Across all of it we kept seeing the same pattern — a customer would find the business, send a message, and the message would get lost. Ad budget was spent and the lead sat in an unread chat.

So we built Bidar: a CRM and unified inbox for WhatsApp, Divar and Bale that sends the first reply and turns every conversation into a trackable lead. Today Bidar is the layer that keeps marketing results from leaking away.

We now run two connected paths: projects and growth retainers for businesses that need implementation, and Bidar subscriptions for teams that simply want their responses and sales organised.

How we operate

These four rules decide which work we take on and which we decline.

Numbers before ideas

Before any proposal we ask how many leads you get today and from where. Without measurement, every change is a guess.

We do not sell what you do not need

If your bottleneck is not the website, we will not sell you a website. We say which change matters most.

Handover includes training

Whatever we build has to run without us. Training your team is part of the project, not an add-on.

No invented numbers

In case studies we only publish numbers that came out of a client dashboard. The rest is shown as [X].
